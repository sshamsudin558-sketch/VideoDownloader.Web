Copyright / DMCA

Last Updated: August 2026

VideoDownloader respects copyright and intellectual property rights. We expect users of the service to respect the rights of content creators and copyright owners.

Important:

VideoDownloader does not claim ownership of third-party content accessed through URLs submitted by users.

1. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the legal right or appropriate permission to download or use any content submitted to VideoDownloader.

You must not use our service to infringe copyright, trademarks, intellectual property rights, or other legal rights.

2. Copyright Complaints

If you believe that content associated with our service infringes your copyright, you may contact us with sufficient information for us to review the complaint.

3. Information to Include

A copyright complaint should contain, where applicable:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  2. Identification of the relevant URL or content.
  3. Your name and contact information.
  4. A statement explaining why you believe the content infringes your copyright.
  5. A statement confirming that the information in your complaint is accurate.
  6. Your electronic or physical signature, where legally required.

4. Review of Complaints

Copyright complaints will be reviewed based on the information provided and applicable law.

We may request additional information when necessary to evaluate a complaint.

5. Counter Notification

If appropriate and where required by applicable law, a person affected by a copyright complaint may be provided with an opportunity to respond or submit a counter notification.

6. Repeat Infringement

Where appropriate, we may restrict or terminate access to users who repeatedly use the service for copyright infringement.

7. No Ownership of Third-Party Content

VideoDownloader does not host or claim ownership of third-party videos merely because a user submits a URL for processing.

Users remain responsible for complying with the applicable terms and laws governing the content they access.

8. Contact for Copyright Matters

Copyright complaints and related requests should be sent through the contact information provided on our Contact page.
