Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to VideoDownloader. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and video downloading service.

VideoDownloader allows users to submit video URLs and request information about publicly accessible online videos.

When you submit a video URL to VideoDownloader, the URL is sent to our server so that the requested video information can be processed.
